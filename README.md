## 🌟 Overview
This is a **personal portfolio website** built with **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**.  
It showcases all your projects, skills, and experiences in a clean and professional way. Perfect for sharing your work online.

## ⚡ Features
- 🏠 **Home Page** – Introduction and personal information.
- 💼 **Projects** – Display all your projects with images, descriptions, and links.
- 📜 **Skills & Experience** – Highlight your technical skills and achievements.
- ✉️ **Contact Form** – Simple form to get in touch.
- 📱 **Responsive Design** – Works well on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
