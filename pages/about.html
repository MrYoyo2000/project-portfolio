ABOUT

My name is Yossef. I grew up with a strong curiosity for how things work especially in the world of technology numbers and systems. From a young age I’ve always been someone who analyzes questions and tries to understand the deeper logic behind everything. This mindset naturally guided me toward fields where structure reasoning and strategy play a central role.

I first chose to study finance a discipline that taught me rigor discipline market analysis and long-term strategic thinking. Throughout my studies I developed a solid understanding of economic behavior risk management and how financial systems evolve and react to global events. Finance didn’t only shape my academic background it shaped my vision of the world: a world driven by patterns logic decisions and anticipation.

During my years in finance I discovered programming. What started as curiosity quickly became a true passion. I began coding independently exploring different technologies and trying to understand how software systems algorithms and blockchain mechanisms work internally. I created small applications experimented with blockchain concepts and built my own tools related to trading and automation. Coding became not only a skill for me but a way of thinking structured analytical creative and solution-oriented.

Today I am pursuing full-stack development with the same determination that has guided me since the beginning. I enjoy learning fast understanding technologies in depth and building clean optimized systems. I combine my background in finance with my technical development skills to create projects that are modern efficient and meaningful. Whether it's building websites exploring blockchain coding trading tools or experimenting with new technologies I approach every project with precision curiosity and long-term vision.

My goal is simple: to continue evolving mastering both technology and strategy and building systems that reflect who I am driven analytical creative and always focused on improvement and progress.

Yossef Benamu

Full Stack Developer | Finance Trader

Passionate about blockchain, web development, and financial technology.
